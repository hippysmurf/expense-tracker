# How to Reset Your Password

> **For developers**: See the [technical implementation guide](../../dev/password-reset-implementation.md)

## What is password reset?

If you've forgotten your password, you can request a reset link to be sent to your email address, then use that link to set a new password and regain access to your account.

## Before You Start

- [ ] You need access to the email address registered to your account
- [ ] Your reset link will expire after **1 hour** — complete the process promptly

---

## Step-by-Step Instructions

### Step 1 — Go to the Forgot Password page

![Step 1: The login page with a "Forgot password?" link highlighted](_screenshots/password-reset-step-1.png)

On the login page, click the **"Forgot password?"** link below the sign-in button.

---

### Step 2 — Enter your email address

![Step 2: The forgot-password form with an email input field](_screenshots/password-reset-step-2.png)

Type the email address associated with your account and click **"Send reset link"**.

You will see a confirmation message. If an account exists for that email, a reset link will be sent within a few minutes. Check your spam/junk folder if it doesn't arrive.

---

### Step 3 — Open the email and click the reset link

![Step 3: Example reset email with a prominent "Reset your password" button](_screenshots/password-reset-step-3.png)

Open the email from the app and click the **"Reset your password"** button (or copy/paste the link into your browser).

> The link expires in 1 hour. If it has expired, return to the Forgot Password page and request a new one.

---

### Step 4 — Enter your new password

![Step 4: The reset-password form with two password fields](_screenshots/password-reset-step-4.png)

Enter your new password in both fields and click **"Update password"**.

---

### Step 5 — Confirm success

![Step 5: Success message confirming password was updated, with a link to log in](_screenshots/password-reset-step-5.png)

You'll see a success message: **"Password updated successfully."** Click **"Log in"** to sign in with your new password.

---

## Common Problems

**"I didn't receive the reset email"**
Check your spam or junk folder. If it's not there after a few minutes, make sure you entered the correct email address and try again.

**"This link has expired or already been used"**
Reset links are valid for 1 hour and can only be used once. Return to the Forgot Password page and request a new link.

**"Passwords do not match"**
Make sure the same password is entered in both fields — they are case-sensitive.

**"Password does not meet requirements"**
Your new password must be at least 8 characters long. Try a longer passphrase or include a mix of letters, numbers, and symbols.

**Nothing happened after I clicked "Send reset link"**
Ensure JavaScript is enabled in your browser and try refreshing the page. If the problem persists, contact support.

---

## Tips

- Use a password manager to generate and store a strong, unique password.
- After resetting, any other active sessions on other devices may be logged out automatically.
